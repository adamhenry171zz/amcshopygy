Qualifying shipments weighing 5 lbs and above may receive free delivery within designated Georgetown delivery areas. Smaller shipments and other locations receive a location-based quote.

Regular delivery hours are Monday–Saturday, 11:00 AM–5:00 PM. After 5 PM and Sunday delivery may carry an additional fee and require special arrangement.
