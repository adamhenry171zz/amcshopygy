Migration safety rules

The October–November 2026 customer-renewal records qualify as active. Later future renewal dates also qualify, including advance-paid subscriptions. The snapshot reference is November 4, 2026.

Important: Column DATE is the customer's renewal date. ACCOUNT PAYMENT DATE is Netflix's provider-side renewal and is not a customer payment obligation. We do not create historical payment transactions from these dates.

This tool never sends the Netflix Password, Gmail Password, PIN, payment-history notes, or unrelated spreadsheet columns to HENRY OS. Only approved customer, plan, amount, and renewal information is saved to the private Streaming store. It does not change the Google Sheet.
