How it works

  1. Ask AMC Shopping for your receipt number.
  2. Enter it above and tap Unlock your form.
  3. Fill in your name, address, Amazon order, and package information.
  4. Download a separate PDF for each Amazon order.
  5. Use the email guide below to send the PDF(s) to Amazon.

Final step · Send your request

How to email Amazon

  1. Open Gmail, Outlook, or the email service linked to your Amazon account. Send from the same email address used for your Amazon purchases.
  2. Compose a new message to tax-exempt@amazon.com (the address shown in AMC's previous example).
  3. Use the subject and short message below.
  4. Attach each Amazon order PDF you downloaded, then send.
  5. Watch your email for Amazon's response. Amazon may request additional documentation or decline a refund.
