## Card treatment

- Full wordmarks wherever space allows; no fake initials such as `D+H`, `P+`, `DZ`, `SZ` or generic `BET+` boxes.
- The Disney+ / Hulu / HBO Max bundle uses all three real logos in one balanced lockup.
- Standardized logo container height so wide and square marks feel visually balanced.
- Badges visible above the product name: `PRIVATE`, `SHARED`, `VPN INCLUDED`, `VPN REQUIRED`, and warranty term.
- Shared-account cards explicitly state that customer credentials/security settings must not be changed.
- HMA VPN is presented as a streaming add-on and a standalone VPN option.

## Christmas mode direction

- Keep AMC navy/electric-blue brand identity.
- Replace the expired website relaunch promotion with a seasonal strip.
- One-time Santa + reindeer fly-across after page load, not a looping distraction.
- Santa can carry an AMC package to make the animation brand-specific.
- Very subtle snow/sparkle layer.
- Smaller/faster animation on mobile.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` and disable motion when requested by the visitor's device.

## Deployment gate

Nothing in this review branch should be merged to `main` or added to the Netlify production build until the owner explicitly approves the final streaming layout and logo treatment.
