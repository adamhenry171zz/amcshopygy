HOW THE WEEKLY CYCLE WORKS

A simple rule customers can use.

What happens after delivery?

A carrier showing Delivered in New York does not mean the parcel is ready for collection in Guyana.

1
Delivered to AMC USACarrier completes delivery.
2
AMC processingPackage is identified, weighed and prepared.
3
In transit to GuyanaIncluded in the outgoing air-freight cycle.
4
Arrived / clearingShipment reaches Guyana and clears.
5
Ready for collection / deliveryAMC sends the final handover information.
